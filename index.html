Community Powered · Real World First · Web3 Enabled

Daily Bread is a food focused platform built around a daily subscription model designed to solve a real problem which is access to consistent meals. Customers subscribe for a fixed daily fee of 9,999 naira and receive breakfast lunch and dinner as a complete package. This model removes daily food stress while creating predictability for both users and operations.

Daily Bread goes beyond being a regular food service. It combines food delivery community engagement and a built in reward system that allows active users to earn while they eat. Through our app users can complete simple tasks engage with the community and earn rewards that directly support their meals. This makes Daily Bread not just a restaurant but an engagement hub where food technology and community intersect.

The vision of Daily Bread is simple but powerful which is to reduce hunger by making food access sustainable and inclusive. We believe that if you have a mobile phone you should be able to earn eat and still save.
By combining daily food access with digital rewards and community participation Daily Bread creates a system where people are not just spending on food but are also building value through everyday activity.

The traditional food and restaurant industry has a major gap which is the absence of community ownership and participation. Communities help businesses grow by buying their products sharing referrals and driving visibility yet they gain nothing beyond consumption. At the same time many people struggle to afford consistent meals even though they are active online daily.

There is also a disconnect between effort and reward. People spend time online every day engaging sharing and contributing value but this effort rarely translates into something tangible like food security. The bridge between earning and eating has been broken and Daily Bread exists to reconnect it.

Daily Bread introduces an onchain restaurant model that combines real world food operations with digital systems that reward participation and transparency. The platform is designed to feel simple to use while powerful in impact.

It brings together real food preparation and distribution blockchain powered transparency that ensures fairness community participation that allows users to earn and contribute and a smooth user experience that does not require technical knowledge. The technology stays in the background while users focus on eating engaging and earning.

Food is universal and non speculative. Everyone understands value when it feeds them and real demand exists every single day regardless of market conditions. Unlike many digital products food does not depend on hype or trends to remain relevant.

Food also serves as the most natural bridge between Web3 and non crypto users. You do not need to understand blockchain to understand hunger. By starting with food Daily Bread introduces people to digital rewards and blockchain powered systems in a way that feels natural familiar and useful.

Daily Bread uses food as the entry point for real world blockchain adoption.

Daily Bread operates through a mobile application that serves as the core access point for users. Through the app customers can place food orders complete tasks earn rewards and claim benefits directly.

There are no traditional physical restaurant locations at this stage. Instead Daily Bread operates with trained chefs and small kitchens strategically positioned across multiple locations to allow faster delivery and better distribution. This decentralized kitchen model improves efficiency while keeping costs low.

The platform also includes digital payment and reward systems a community engagement and governance layer and analytics dashboards that provide transparency and operational insights. All technology remains invisible to everyday users while ensuring trust and accountability behind the scene

Road Map

The first phase focuses on concept validation and community building. This includes establishing the brand defining operational processes launching the landing page and onboarding early supporters.

Validation and Community Building

Phase 1

This phase runs for approximately 21 to 30 days and involves a pilot food test. The first 50 customers are onboarded daily food delivery is tested data is collected and user behavior is analyzed. A structured waitlist is also created during this phase.

Food Test

Phase 2

Funding is secured to build the full application train chefs and map out food hotspot locations. University tours are conducted across Nigeria Liberia and Kenya to introduce Daily Bread and onboard users. UNIGOX is introduced as the crypto payment method and reward claiming system. This phase serves as an extended pilot.

Preparation

Phase 3

The final phase marks the official launch of the application with a fair and transparent reward distribution system. Thousands of users are onboarded and Daily Bread begins operating at full scale.
